Build a complete, modern mobile-first web app called Phone Business Manager for managing a small phone-reselling business in Zambia.

The app should be designed for a single business owner who buys smartphones, mainly iPhones, imports them, pays shipping, sells them locally, and wants to track inventory, costs, sales, profit, cash flow, and business growth without using pen and paper.

1. Main goal

The app must make it extremely easy for me to:

1. Add phones I purchase.
2. Track every phone individually.
3. Automatically calculate the true landed cost of each phone.
4. Track phones currently in stock.
5. Record sales.
6. Automatically calculate gross profit.
7. Track business capital separately from profit.
8. Track expenses such as shipping, repairs, transport and accessories.
9. See how much money is available for buying more stock.
10. Track progress from 5 phones → 10 → 20 → 50 phones.
11. View useful business statistics from a dashboard.
12. Avoid accidentally spending money that is required to replace my inventory.

The app should use Zambian Kwacha (ZMW/K) as the default currency.

7. Shipping allocation

Shipping may sometimes apply to an entire batch rather than one phone.

Create a feature for recording a shipment/batch.

Example:

I buy 5 phones and pay K1,000 shipping for the entire batch.

The app should allow me to enter:

Batch shipping = K1,000

Then allocate that K1,000 across the phones.

Default method:

Shipping per phone = Total shipping ÷ Number of phones

For 5 phones:

K1,000 ÷ 5 = K200 per phone.

Then each phone’s landed cost includes its K200 shipping allocation.

Allow the user to manually adjust the allocation if needed.

22. Dashboard calculations

All dashboard calculations must come from database records.

Never hard-code business numbers.

At minimum calculate:

Total inventory cost

= Sum of landed costs of all unsold phones.

Potential revenue

= Sum of expected selling prices of all unsold phones.

Potential profit

= Potential revenue − inventory cost.

Total revenue

= Sum of completed sales.

Realized gross profit

= Sum of actual sale price − landed cost for sold phones.

Total expenses

= Sum of recorded business expenses.

Personal withdrawals

= Sum of withdrawals.

27. Important business logic

Do not oversimplify the accounting.

There are three different concepts:

Inventory cost

What I have invested into phones that I still own.

Revenue

Money received from selling phones.

Profit

Revenue minus the cost of the phones sold and relevant expenses.

Do not calculate profit simply as:

“Current cash − original investment.”

Use proper transaction-based calculations.
