STRICT BUSINESS MONEY GUARD

Build a strict financial discipline system called Business Money Guard.

Its purpose is to prevent me from accidentally spending money that should remain in the phone business.

This feature should be visible on the Dashboard and have its own dedicated page.

1. The three-money system

The app must separate my money into:

PROTECTED STOCK CAPITAL
Money required to maintain/replenish my phone inventory.

SAFE-TO-SPEND PROFIT
Money that the business has genuinely earned and that I can potentially withdraw or spend.

EMERGENCY BUSINESS RESERVE
A configurable amount that I want to keep available for unexpected costs such as repairs, shipping increases, returns, or other business problems.

Never combine these three numbers.

⸻

2. Strict spending calculator

Create a calculator where I enter:

* Current business cash
* Current inventory cost
* Number of phones in stock
* Average landed cost per phone
* Target number of phones
* Emergency reserve
* Amount I want to spend

The app calculates:

Protected Stock Capital

= Target inventory requirement based on the configured target and/or the amount required to replace current stock.

Safe-to-Spend Amount

= Business cash − Protected Stock Capital − Emergency Reserve

If the result is below zero:

Safe-to-Spend Amount = K0

Do not allow the app to display a negative amount as money I can spend.

⸻

3. Strict decision

When I enter an amount I want to spend, give me a very clear result.

If I can afford it:

✅ SAFE TO SPEND

Example:

You can spend up to K2,500.

Planned spending: K1,500

Remaining safe-to-spend amount: K1,000

If I cannot afford it:

🛑 DO NOT SPEND

Example:

DO NOT SPEND K3,000

Safe-to-spend amount: K1,200

You would exceed your safe spending limit by K1,800.

Your stock capital would be affected.

The warning should be prominent and impossible to misunderstand.

⸻

4. Spending categories

When recording spending, ask what the money is for:

* New phone stock
* Shipping
* Repairs
* Accessories
* Advertising
* Transport
* Business equipment
* Personal spending
* Other

Personal spending must never automatically reduce the protected stock capital calculation without showing the consequences.

⸻

5. “Can I afford this?” button

Add a large button:

CAN I AFFORD THIS?

When pressed, show a simple calculator.

Example:

Business cash: K17,400

Protected stock capital: K10,212

Emergency reserve: K1,000

Safe to spend: K6,188

Then:

I want to spend: K5,000

Result:

✅ YES — within your safe spending limit.

Remaining safe-to-spend money: K1,188

If I enter K8,000:

🛑 NO — DO NOT SPEND.

Safe-to-spend: K6,188

Amount over limit: K1,812

⸻

6. Strict mode

Add a setting called:

STRICT MODE

When enabled:

* Always show the protected amount.
* Always show the maximum safe spending amount.
* Warn before personal withdrawals.
* Warn before business purchases that could reduce stock capital.
* Require confirmation for spending above the safe limit.
* Never call protected stock capital “profit.”
* Never count unsold inventory as cash.
* Never count expected profit as spendable money.

The app should use strong but professional language such as:

“STOP — THIS MONEY IS PROTECTED FOR STOCK.”

and:

“You can spend K____ without reducing your protected business capital.”

⸻

7. Reinvestment recommendation

Create a separate calculation:

NEXT STOCK ORDER

Show:

* Current cash
* Protected capital
* Available profit
* Average landed cost
* Maximum phones affordable
* Recommended amount to reinvest
* Cash remaining after purchase

Example:

Current business cash: K17,400

Protected capital: K10,212

Safe-to-spend profit: K7,188

Average landed cost: K2,042

Maximum additional phones: 3

Cost of 3 phones: approximately K6,126

Remaining cash: approximately K11,274

All figures must be calculated dynamically from actual database information.

⸻

8. Profit withdrawal calculator

Create a button:

HOW MUCH CAN I TAKE OUT?

Calculate the maximum amount I can withdraw while maintaining:

1. Required stock capital.
2. Emergency reserve.
3. Any outstanding business expenses.

Show:

Maximum safe withdrawal: K____

Then show:

If you withdraw K____, your business will have K____ remaining after protecting stock capital and your emergency reserve.

⸻

9. Business discipline score

Do NOT create a fake financial “credit score.”

Instead, show simple factual indicators:

Stock Capital Protected: YES/NO

Emergency Reserve Maintained: YES/NO

Spending Within Limit: YES/NO

Next Stock Target: K____

Amount Needed: K____

Do not use arbitrary scores such as “8/10 financial discipline.”

⸻

10. Important calculation rules

The calculator must distinguish between:

Cash = money actually available.

Inventory = phones currently owned.

Revenue = money received from sales.

Gross Profit = revenue minus the landed cost of phones sold.

Protected Capital = money reserved to keep the business operating and replace inventory.

Safe-to-Spend = cash remaining after protected capital and emergency reserve.

Expected Profit from unsold phones is NOT spendable cash.

Never tell me I can spend money simply because my inventory has a high potential selling value.

⸻

11. Example using my current business

Use this only as an example, not hard-coded data:

I purchased:

* 2 iPhone X
* 3 iPhone 11

Purchase cost:

K9,212

Estimated shipping:

K1,000

Estimated landed cost:

K10,212

Expected sales:

K17,400

Expected gross profit:

K7,188

If all phones are still unsold, the app must NOT tell me that K7,188 is currently spendable profit.

It should recognize that the K10,212 is tied up in inventory.

After phones are actually sold, the app should update the real cash and realized profit automatically.

⸻

12. Goal

The purpose of this system is simple:

Make it difficult for me to accidentally eat into my business capital.

Every time I consider spending money, the app should answer:

“How much can I safely spend?”

and:

“How much must remain protected for the business?”

The calculations must be transparent so I can see exactly how the app arrived at the number.
