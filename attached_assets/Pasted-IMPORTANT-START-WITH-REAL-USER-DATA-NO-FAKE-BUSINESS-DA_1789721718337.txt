IMPORTANT: START WITH REAL USER DATA — NO FAKE BUSINESS DATA

Do NOT populate the production app with fake phones, fake sales, fake profit, or demo financial figures.

When I first open the application, my inventory should be empty unless I have entered real information.

The application should have a setup process where I can enter my REAL current business information.

Do not assume that I own any particular number of phones.

Do not assume my starting capital.

Do not assume my purchase prices.

Do not assume my selling prices.

All business calculations must be based only on information I actually enter.

⸻

PHONE ACQUISITION SYSTEM

A phone can enter my inventory in different ways.

When I add a phone, first ask:

How did you get this phone?

Options:

1. Purchased
2. Free / Gift
3. Trade-in
4. Other

⸻

1. PURCHASED PHONE

If I select Purchased, show:

* Purchase price
* Shipping
* Customs/import costs
* Repairs
* Accessories
* Other costs

Calculate:

Total Cost = Purchase Price + Shipping + Customs + Repairs + Accessories + Other Costs

Then allow me to enter:

Expected Selling Price

Calculate:

Expected Profit = Expected Selling Price − Total Cost

⸻

2. FREE / GIFT PHONE

This is very important.

Sometimes I may receive a phone for free.

For example:

iPhone X

Acquisition type:

Free / Gift

Purchase price:

K0

Shipping:

K0

Repairs:

K0

Expected selling price:

K2,700

The system should calculate:

Actual Cash Cost = K0

Expected Sale = K2,700

Expected Gross Profit = K2,700

However, the app must clearly distinguish between:

Cash invested: K0

and

Potential value/profit: K2,700

Do not pretend that a free phone cost me money.

If I later spend K300 repairing the phone, then:

Actual Cost = K300

and if I sell it for K2,700:

Gross Profit = K2,400

All calculations must update automatically.

⸻

3. TRADE-IN

Allow a phone to be acquired through a trade.

For example:

I give someone:

Old phone valued at K1,500

and receive:

iPhone 11

The app should allow me to record the value assigned to what I gave up.

Then calculate the phone’s acquisition cost based on the trade value plus any additional costs.

Do not treat this as a normal cash purchase.

Clearly label:

Acquisition method: Trade-in

⸻

4. OTHER

Allow custom acquisition methods.

For example:

* Business transfer
* Prize
* Family transfer
* Other

Allow the user to enter the appropriate cost/value manually.

⸻

EXPECTED SELLING PRICE VS ACTUAL SELLING PRICE

Every phone should have two separate fields:

Expected Selling Price

What I currently think I can sell the phone for.

Actual Selling Price

The amount I actually sold it for.

Do NOT replace the expected selling price with the actual selling price automatically.

Keep both values in the database.

Before sale:

Show:

Expected Sale: K4,000

Expected Profit: K1,958

After sale:

Show:

Actual Sale: K3,800

Actual Profit: K1,758

The actual sale should be used for realized revenue and realized profit.

⸻

PRICE UPDATE FEATURE

I should be able to change the expected selling price at any time.

Example:

I originally enter:

iPhone 11

Expected selling price: K4,000

Later I decide the market price is only K3,700.

I should be able to edit:

Expected Selling Price → K3,700

The app immediately recalculates:

Expected Profit

and:

Expected Profit Margin

This should NOT change the original acquisition cost.

⸻

INVENTORY VALUE

Do not confuse inventory value with cash.

For every phone, show:

Acquisition Cost

Expected Selling Price

Expected Profit

The dashboard can calculate:

Potential Inventory Revenue

but must NOT add that potential revenue to my actual business cash.

For example:

If I have a free iPhone worth K4,000:

Inventory:

1 phone

Potential revenue:

K4,000

Actual cash:

K0

The app must not say I have K4,000 cash.

⸻

STARTUP / INITIAL SETUP

When I first open the app, show:

“Let’s set up your real business”

Ask:

How much business cash do you currently have?

How many phones do you currently own?

Then allow me to add each existing phone individually.

For every existing phone I should be able to choose:

* Purchased
* Free/Gift
* Trade-in
* Other

I should then enter its actual information.

Do not create sample phones automatically.

⸻

RESET DEMO DATA

Do not include fake demo data in the real business database.

If you want to demonstrate the interface, create a completely separate:

“Try Demo Mode”

button.

Demo Mode must use a separate temporary dataset and must never mix with my real business data.

The real business mode must always start with my actual information.

⸻

STRICT MONEY CALCULATOR UPDATE

The Business Money Guard must also understand free phones.

A free phone with K0 acquisition cost should NOT cause the app to reserve money that was never spent.

However, if I decide that I need K500 for repairs, the app should reserve/account for that K500.

The calculator must always distinguish:

Cash actually invested

from:

Potential selling value

and:

Expected profit.

⸻

IMPORTANT

The app is a business accounting and inventory tool.

Never invent financial information.

Never assume a phone was purchased.

Never assume a selling price.

Never assume shipping.

Never assume profit.

Everything must come from the user’s actual entries.

The user must be able to edit every important financial value.

The app should show exactly how each calculation was produced so the business owner can trust the numbers.
