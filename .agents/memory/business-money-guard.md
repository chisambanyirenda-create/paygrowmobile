---
name: Business Money Guard
description: Durable rules for protecting phone-business cash from accidental spending
---

The money guard must keep protected stock capital, safe-to-spend cash, and the emergency business reserve as separate figures. Safe-to-spend cash is never negative:

`max(0, actual cash - protected stock capital - emergency reserve)`

Unsold inventory value and potential profit are not spendable cash. Above-limit spending must be clearly labeled as unsafe and require an explicit confirmation before it can be recorded.

**Why:** The product exists to prevent personal or incidental spending from consuming the money needed to replace phone inventory.

**How to apply:** Preserve these labels and calculation boundaries in any future money, dashboard, withdrawal, expense, or growth feature.
